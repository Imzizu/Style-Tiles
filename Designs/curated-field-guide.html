Curated Field Guide

A tactile, utilitarian design language inspired by physical field notebooks, artisanal travel gazettes, and curated expedition journals. Built on a warm linen parchment baseline (#FAF8F5), muted sand sub-surfaces (#F2EFEB), high-contrast matte charcoal CTA buttons (#121212), and bold geometric display numerals in warm saffron amber (#E5A42E) and burnt terracotta (#DE5333).

44px Number Column Hairline Dividers Micro Badges Zero Blur Baseline
FIELD SPEC // REGISTRY 2026-09-01 UTC

Construction Philosophy: Flat paper substrate without heavy drop shadows. Visual depth is achieved exclusively through high-contrast floating pill CTAs, 3px indicator lines, and geometric step numerals.
