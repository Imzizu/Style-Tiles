Neo-Swiss Editorial

Modernist Monochrome Architecture

Rooted in mid-century International Typographic Style and contemporary Zurich editorial design, this modernist monochrome framework rejects ornamental depth in favor of rigorous structural geometry. Dual-canvas planes of pristine white (#FFFFFF) and cool architectural stone (#E5E5E7) create spatial tension without a single drop shadow, accented by hairline tabular dividing rules and high-impact vermilion focal signals.
