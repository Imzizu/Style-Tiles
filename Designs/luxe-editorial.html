Essay 01 · The Cut

Paper & Pitch

Cover story

Monochrome is the luxury

Color is a concession. This system keeps the folio on bleached stock and Didot ink so photography — even when simulated in gradient and grain — can carry the drama. Frosted glass is the only overlay: a caption, a credit, a play glyph inverted in a black disc.

Use it for lookbooks, house journals, and archival fashion sites that must feel printed, not productized.
